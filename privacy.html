Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Who we are

Kobani Plumbers is a gas, heating and plumbing business based at 133 Cumbrian Gardens, London NW2 1EJ. You can reach us on 07727 290739. This policy explains how we look after your personal information, whether you visit this website or contact us for work.

What this website collects

This website does not collect personal information. It has no account, no contact form and no analytics, and we do not set cookies.

The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, your browser sends your IP address and basic device details to Google, as it does for any site that uses them. Google's own privacy policy covers that. The Google Maps link and the MyBuilder reviews take you to those companies' sites, which have their own policies.

If you contact us

When you call, message or book us, we may collect:

  • your name and phone number
  • the address of the property we are working at
  • details of the problem, including any photos you send us
  • payment details for the job, and gas safety records where we carry out gas work

We use this to give you a quote, arrange visits, carry out and invoice the work, answer questions afterwards, and meet our legal duties, including gas safety record keeping. We do this because we need it to carry out our agreement with you, because the law requires it, or because it is a reasonable part of running our business.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only where it is needed to do the job or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as we need them to carry out the work, deal with any follow-up, and meet legal, tax and gas safety record requirements. After that we delete them.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete your information, or restrict how we use it
  • stop using it for something you object to
  • give you a copy of it in a portable form

To make a request, call us on 07727 290739 or write to the address above. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
